## description

A little prayer. A friend along the way.

Meet Capy, a friendly capybara who helps your child practice talking to God, one small moment at a time. Made for Christian families with children ages 4–8, CapyPray brings together guided prayers, illustrated Bible stories, and gentle play.

PRAY TOGETHER
Listen to Capy, repeat a short prayer, and tap when you're ready. Practice gratitude, kindness, saying sorry, asking for help, and praying for people you love. Children can speak out loud; CapyPray does not record their voice.

A PEACEFUL BEDTIME ROUTINE
Make room for a quiet prayer at the end of the day. Bedtime prayers remain free, with optional reminders set by a grown-up.

BIBLE STORIES TO EXPLORE
Discover illustrated stories with narration and small moments of interaction, made for reading and listening together.

LITTLE MOMENTS OF PLAY
Enjoy four puzzle games: Color Pour, Block Garden, Triple Tiles, and Memory Pond. Practice matching, sorting, and solving at your own pace.

WATCH CAPY'S WORLD GROW
Follow a prayer journey, light lanterns, and discover treasures as you progress. Come back when you're ready: Capy is glad to see you.

MADE WITH FAMILIES IN MIND
• No ads or open chat
• No microphone recording
• A parental gate before subscriptions and grown-up settings
• English prayers and narration
• Progress stored on your device

FREE AND PREMIUM
Start with free prayer moments, including bedtime prayer. An optional monthly or annual subscription unlocks the full guided prayer curriculum.
