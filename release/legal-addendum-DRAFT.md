## Privacy text proposal

CapyPray is a Christian prayer and play app for children ages 4–8 and their parents, operated by Looplab Studio. This section applies to CapyPray and is separate from our website privacy practices.

The app stores a child's nickname, age band, prayer progress, game levels, preferences, and the labels or notes a family chooses for prayer people on the device. Please use nicknames and avoid sensitive details. In the initial store release these profiles are not uploaded to a Looplab account or cloud backup. The app does not provide account creation. Parents can erase this local profile in Parent Corner. Deleting the profile does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription.

We do not record a child's voice, collect photos or precise location, read contacts, display advertisements, or provide open chat. Spoken prayers are not sent to an AI service. Pre-recorded narration is included with the app. Optional bedtime reminders use the device's local notification service, subject to a parent's permission.

Subscriptions are for adults and accessed through a parental gate. Apple or Google processes payment. RevenueCat helps us validate purchases, restore access, and keep subscription status current using a pseudonymous app user identifier and transaction information. It also receives the technical data necessary to provide its service. We do not send children's names, prayer text, prayer-people labels, or progress to RevenueCat. Advertising identifier collection is disabled. We do not use Firebase/GA4 or advertising SDKs inside this release.

Parents may contact hello@looplab.studio to ask about data handled by Looplab and its service providers, including access or deletion requests. Do not send sensitive child information in support messages. Records that must be retained for accounting or legal purposes may be kept as required. CapyPray is intended for family use with age-appropriate adult involvement. A parent or legal guardian must manage subscriptions. Access to the free features does not require a subscription. Premium unlocks the guided curriculum described in the app; cosmetic rewards still depend on progress.

Subscriptions renew automatically for the billing period shown by your store, unless canceled before renewal. Any introductory offer depends on eligibility determined by Apple or Google; the store confirmation sheet displays the applicable price and terms. Manage or cancel in your store subscription settings. Deleting the app or local profile does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled under the applicable store's rules.

Content is for Christian prayer and family learning; it is not professional medical or mental-health advice. The initial release supports English and one local child profile. Local progress may be lost when the app is deleted or device data is erased. We do not promise cross-platform progress or family profile synchronization.
