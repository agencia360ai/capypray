The app is designed for children ages 4–8 praying together with a parent. We built it to collect as little as possible. This policy is our notice to parents under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## What we do NOT collect
- No names, emails, phone numbers, photos, videos, drawings, voice recordings or location from your child.
- No advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android Advertising ID). No ads of any kind. No tracking across other apps or websites.
- No chat, no social features, no contact with strangers. Capy's lines are pre-recorded; there is no AI talking to your child.

## What stays on your device only
The nickname you type for your child, the bedtime you choose, the "prayer people" your child adds, and your child's progress (lessons, lanterns, streaks) are stored **only on your device**. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them; you can also clear them in Parent Corner (behind the parental gate).

## What we collect, and why
**Anonymous usage statistics.** To understand which parts of the app work and fix what doesn't, the app sends anonymous events to Google Analytics (Google LLC acts as our service provider), for example "lesson completed", "game level won", "screen opened", app version and platform (iOS/Android). Each installation gets a random identifier created by the app; it is not linked to your child, to you, or to your device's advertising identifier. We never send your child's nickname, answers typed by you, or any free text. We use this data only to maintain and improve the app (the "support for internal operations" exception under COPPA), never for advertising or profiling. Google Signals, ad personalization and granular location/device data collection are turned off, and data is kept for [2] months.

**Purchases.** Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider to confirm whether a subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user id and the store's purchase receipt — no name, email or device identifiers.

## Notifications
If you turn on the bedtime reminder in Parent Corner, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No push service receives your information.

## Parental gate
Purchases, settings, links out of the app and Parent Corner are behind a parental gate so that only a grown-up can reach them.

## Your choices and rights
You can review or delete your child's information at any time: all of it lives on your device (Parent Corner → delete, or delete the app). Because analytics are anonymous, we cannot link them to your child; if you still want us to delete analytics data or have questions, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] and we will respond within [30] days.

## Changes
If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and, where required, ask for your consent before collecting anything new.
